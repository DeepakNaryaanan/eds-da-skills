    Bundled Repair & Maintenance Plans

    Maintain peak performance and extend your instruments life with an annual preventive maintenance service included in your plan. All field personnel use high quality Agilent parts and supplies, and follow a pre-defined checklist for the highest levels of maintenance. Troubleshoot instrument issues with our Agilent Certified service professionals using virtual technical support. If an onsite repair is required, benefit from preferred response time and a same-day fix rate of 80%-- so you can get back to the science.

    Choose from a variety of bundled repair and maintenance service plans including parts, travel, labor, and consumables required for repair.
