Living Well with DUOPA

DUOPA (carbidopa and levodopa enteral suspension) delivers continuous levodopa therapy directly to the small intestine, helping to reduce unpredictable "off" periods for people with advanced Parkinson's disease.

How DUOPA Works

DUOPA is delivered through a portable pump connected to a tube that passes through the abdomen and into the upper small intestine. The pump supplies a continuous, steady dose of levodopa throughout the day, smoothing out the fluctuations that oral pills can cause.

Benefits of Continuous Delivery

In clinical studies, patients experienced on average four fewer hours of "off" time per day compared to oral medication. Continuous delivery helps maintain stable drug levels, reducing motor fluctuations and improving quality of life.

Important Safety Information

DUOPA is a prescription medicine. Before starting treatment, talk to your doctor about your medical history, other medications you take, and any known allergies. Not all patients are suitable candidates for this therapy.
