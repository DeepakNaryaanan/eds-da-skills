Clinical Context

Continuous Delivery for Advanced Parkinson's

DUOPA delivers carbidopa and levodopa directly into the small intestine, reducing motor fluctuations and providing up to 16 hours of continuous drug delivery daily.

Learn More About DUOPA

Patient Benefits

More "On" Time. Less Disruption.

In clinical studies, 74% of patients achieved greater "On" time compared to oral levodopa therapy, enabling more daily activity without troublesome dyskinesia.

Read Patient Stories

How It Works

Direct Intestinal Infusion

A portable pump delivers a gel suspension of carbidopa/levodopa continuously through a tube placed directly into the small intestine via a PEG-J procedure.

For Healthcare Providers

Candidate Identification

Consider DUOPA for patients with advanced Parkinson's disease who have motor fluctuations that are not adequately controlled on oral levodopa therapy.
