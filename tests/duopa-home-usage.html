Understanding DUOPA

DUOPA is designed for patients with advanced Parkinson's disease who still experience motor fluctuations despite optimised oral therapy.

How It Works

Continuous Intestinal Delivery

Unlike oral tablets, DUOPA delivers a precisely metered dose of carbidopa/levodopa directly to the small intestine via a portable infusion pump and a PEG-J tube. This bypasses the stomach, reducing variability in drug absorption and smoothing dopamine levels throughout the day.

The pump runs for up to 16 hours per day, maintaining steady medication levels during waking hours.

Clinical Data

Proven Reduction in "Off" Time

In the pivotal double-blind clinical trial (LCIG-2010-01), patients treated with DUOPA experienced a statistically significant reduction in mean daily "Off" time of approximately 4 hours compared to those receiving oral carbidopa/levodopa.

74% of DUOPA-treated patients achieved a greater reduction in "Off" time than the oral treatment group.

Is DUOPA Right for Your Patient?

DUOPA may be appropriate for adults with advanced Parkinson's disease who:

  • Experience at least 3 hours of daily "Off" time despite optimised oral therapy
  • Have a confirmed response to levodopa
  • Are willing and able to manage a PEG-J tube and portable infusion pump

Talk to a movement disorder specialist about whether DUOPA is right for you or your patient. You can also call the DUOPA Support team at 1-800-DUOPA-RX for personalised assistance.
